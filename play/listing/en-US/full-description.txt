OneDoc opens documents, spreadsheets and PDFs right on your phone — no internet, no sign-up and no ads.

Supported formats: PDF, DOCX, DOC, XLSX, XLS, CSV, TXT and XML.

OPEN FILES FROM ANYWHERE
• Attachments from email and messengers: choose "Open with" or "Share" → OneDoc.
• Files from your phone's storage, Downloads and file managers.
• Library: add folders with documents — OneDoc finds the files and keeps the list up to date.

PDF
• Large files open fast.
• Jump to a page with the slider or by number.
• Table of contents.
• Text search with highlighted results.
• Password-protected PDFs.

TEXT DOCUMENTS (DOCX, DOC, TXT, XML)
• Text reflows to fit the screen — no sideways scrolling.
• Choose the font, text size and line spacing.
• Headings, lists, tables and images from DOCX and DOC.
• Table of contents built from headings.
• XML is shown indented with highlighted tags; damaged XML opens as plain text.
• Automatic text encoding detection, including Windows-1251.

SPREADSHEETS (XLSX, XLS, CSV)
• Sheet tabs at the bottom of the screen.
• Numbers, dates and percentages look the same as in the original file.
• Frozen header row.
• Sharp pinch-to-zoom of the grid.
• Search within the cells of a sheet.
• Large spreadsheets appear immediately, before loading finishes.

COMFORTABLE READING
• The app remembers where you stopped and reopens the document at the same place.
• Bookmarks with quick navigation.
• Reading themes: light, dark and sepia.
• Full-screen mode: tap the centre to hide the toolbars.
• Pinch to zoom.

LIBRARY
• Library and Recent tabs.
• List or grid view with PDF thumbnails.
• Instant search by name, filter by type, sort by date, name or size.
• Rename, delete and share right from the list.

PRIVACY
• The app has no internet access — your documents never leave the device.
• No ads, no analytics, no accounts.
• Access only to the folders and files you choose yourself.

Interface in English, Russian and Italian. Works on Android 8.0 and newer, on phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape.
